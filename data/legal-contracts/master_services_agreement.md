# Master Services Agreement (MSA-2026-CLOUD)

## 1. Scope of Managed Cloud Services
The Service Provider agrees to deliver enterprise multi-cloud management, Kubernetes cluster administration, and 24/7 incident response for the Client.

## 2. Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Penalties
The Provider guarantees service availability according to the tier definitions below:

| Service Tier | Guaranteed Uptime | Response Time (P1) | Monthly Credit for SLA Breach |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mission Critical | 99.99% | 15 minutes | 20% of monthly fee |
| Production Standard | 99.90% | 30 minutes | 10% of monthly fee |
| Development / Staging | 99.50% | 2 hours | 5% of monthly fee |

## 3. Data Confidentiality and Security
All client data stored in Object Storage and Managed PostgreSQL databases must be encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3.
Security audits are performed semi-annually by an independent third-party auditor.
